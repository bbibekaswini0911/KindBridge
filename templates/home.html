FAQ

Questions people ask

What is KindBridge?

A platform that connects donors with orphanages, old age homes and care centers.

Is it free to use?

Yes. Registering and sending requests costs nothing.

Do I need an account to donate?

Yes. Register once, then log in to send and track requests.

What can I donate?

Whatever the center lists as a requirement, such as food, clothes, medicine or books.

How do I know my request was accepted?

Your dashboard shows Pending, Approved or Rejected for every request.

How does a care center join?

Register your center. The administrator checks it, and you can log in once it is approved.

Who approves requests?

The care center reviews each request and updates its status.
